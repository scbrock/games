# Mossblade

A top-down action adventure for iPhone, in the spirit of classic 8-bit
adventure games, with its own world, characters, pixel art and chiptune music.
It's one HTML file with no build step and no dependencies.

**Story:** the Cinder Warden has stolen the Sunstone, and Hollowmere Valley is
wilting. Find a blade, open the Ember Ruins, defeat the Warden, and bring the
Sunstone home.

## Controls

- **Left pad:** walk (drag your thumb)
- **A:** swing the sword, or talk, read signs and open chests when facing them
- **B:** drink a potion
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to walk, Z for A, X for B, P to pause

At full health, every sword swing also throws a beam of light.

## The world

A 3×3 overworld of flip screens, two caves and a four-room dungeon:

- **Hermit's Cave** (start screen): Old Tamsin and the Mossblade
- **The Old Graveyard** (west): defeat the gravekeepers for the Old Key
- **Ember Ruins** (north): the dungeon gate; inside, a Bone Hall with the
  Warden Key, a Bat Roost with treasure, and the Warden's Forge boss room
- **Tanglewood** (north-west): a tree maze with a heart vessel
- **Stillwater** (north-east): a treasure chest on an island
- **Moonwell** (south-west): a spring that heals you
- **Pip's Shop** (south-east): potions and a heart vessel for gems

Progress saves automatically on the device, so you can close the app and
continue later.

## Play it on your iPhone

Host this folder on any static host (for example GitHub Pages, then
`https://<user>.github.io/games/mossblade/`). Open it in Safari and tap
**Share → Add to Home Screen** to play full screen.
