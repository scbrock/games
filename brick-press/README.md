# Brick Press

Breakout for iPhone, printed in risograph inks. It's one HTML file with no
build step and no dependencies.

**How to play:** drag anywhere on the playfield to move the paddle. The area
under the paddle is room for your thumb. Tap to launch the ball. Blue bricks
take two hits. Clear every brick to move to the next sheet, which runs a
little faster. There are five layouts, and they repeat with more speed.

Bricks sometimes drop a capsule. Catch it with the paddle:

- **W**: wide paddle for 12 seconds
- **M**: every ball in play splits into three
- **S**: slow ball for 9 seconds

You have three balls. Sound can be muted with the speaker button, and the game
pauses on its own when you switch apps. Your best score is saved on the device.

On a keyboard: the arrow keys move, Space launches, P pauses.

## Play it on your iPhone

Host this folder on any static host (for example GitHub Pages, then
`https://<user>.github.io/games/brick-press/`). Open it in Safari and tap
**Share → Add to Home Screen** to run it full screen.
