Make your iPhone quieter

This app can't block other apps; only iPhone settings can. These steps make Enough the first thing you see, and make everything else take effort. Fifteen minutes, once.

  1. Put Enough on your Home ScreenIn Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Move it to the dock or the first page.
  2. Empty your Home ScreenTouch and hold the background, tap the page dots at the bottom, and untick every page except the one with Enough. Your apps stay in the App Library (swipe left past the last page) and in search.
  3. Make the phone greySettings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters → Grayscale. Then Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut → Color Filters, so a triple-click of the side button switches colour back when you need it.
  4. Limit the pulling appsSettings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit. Pick Social and Entertainment (or single apps), set something like 20 minutes a day, and turn on Block at End of Limit.
  5. Keep the essentials open in DowntimeScreen Time → Downtime: set your evening hours. Then Screen Time → Always Allowed: add Phone, Messages, WhatsApp, Maps. Everything else greys out during Downtime.
  6. Quiet the notificationsSettings → Focus → New Focus → Custom. Allow notifications only from your people and from Messages and WhatsApp. Under Customize Screens, choose the Home Screen page with Enough on it.
  7. Make the limits stickScreen Time → Lock Screen Time Settings, and ask someone you trust to choose the passcode.
