Table talk

    How cribbage works here

    The deal. Two players get 6 cards, everyone else 5. With 5 players the dealer gets 4; with 6, the dealer and the player before them get 4. Players with extra cards throw them to the dealer's crib, so every hand and the crib end up with 4. With 3 players one card from the deck joins the crib.

    The cut. The player after the dealer cuts the starter. A jack scores the dealer 2 ("his heels").

    The play. Take turns playing a card and adding to the count, never past 31. Score 2 for 15 or 31, 2 for a pair (6 for three, 12 for four), and the length of any run. If you can't play, say Go. The last person to play scores 1 (2 if they hit 31), and the count starts again.

    The show. Starting after the dealer, each hand is counted with the starter: 2 for each fifteen, 2 per pair, runs, a flush (4, or 5 with the starter; the crib needs all 5), and 1 for the jack of the starter's suit ("nobs"). The dealer counts last, then the crib. The app counts everything for you.

    Winning. First to 121 wins, the moment they reach it. Finishing below 91 is a skunk; below 61, a double skunk.

    Partnerships. With 4 or 6 players you can play in teams of two, sitting alternately. Partners share one peg.
